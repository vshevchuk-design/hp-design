DateRangePicker

tokens/components/date-range-picker.tokens.json · generated — a period navigator that opens a range calendar. Step the range with the arrows, or click a date to pick a custom start & end; the span between highlights.

Navigator‹ prev · start date · period label · end date · next ›, hairline-divided in the field recipe (surface.dim → border.strong hover → border.focus). The arrows step the whole range by a month; the label reads Current for the default window, else the month.
CalendarFloats on the Popover shell. Click a day to set the start, click again to set the end (picking earlier swaps them). Prev/next month nav, Apply / Clear in the footer.
RangeStart and end fill fill.primary; the days between get a light bg.primary wash; today keeps its hairline ring.

Filter by date window

Default range Aug 9 – Sep 8, 2026 ("Current"); "today" is Aug 8 in this deterministic demo.
