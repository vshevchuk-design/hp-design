Are you looking to stay on track or get ahead in your degree progress? California State University, San Bernardino is pleased to announce the upcoming Winter Intersession.

This compacted term allows you to earn up to 4 units in just a few weeks, helping you fulfill requirements between the Fall and Spring semesters.

Important Details

Dates: December 19, 2025 – January 12, 2026

Format: Accelerated courses held during the winter break.

Eligibility: Open to current CSUSB students and the general public.

Why Consider Winter Intersession?

Fast-Track Success: Complete a full 3 or 4-unit course in less than a month.

Focus Your Energy: Focus intensely on a single challenging subject without the distraction of a full course load.

Lighten Your Spring: Reduce your unit load for the upcoming Spring semester.
