Date Navigator

tokens/components/date-navigator.tokens.json · generated — steps a calendar view through time and names the period on screen: ‹ previous · › next · Today, then the period as a trigger that opens the Calendar to jump to any date.

AnatomyThree Button secondary base (the arrows icon-only), then the period — heading-lg 18/600 with a chevron, a ghost trigger 40px high (Button ghost's hover and focus ring). The period opens the DS Calendar in DatePicker's panel.
Unitsday "Thu, Oct 29, 2026" · week "Oct 25 – 31, 2026" (Sunday-first; across months "Oct 25 – Nov 1, 2026", across years both dates in full) · month "October 2026". The arrows step one unit and say which ("Previous week").
Layoutsinline — controls, then the period (desktop, tablet). split — the period first and flexible, the controls at the far end (a phone row). Under 768px the period drops to heading-md 16/600.
TodayAlways enabled — it is also how you get back to today's position inside a period that already contains it.
Not forChoosing a date for a form — that is DatePicker. This one moves a view; it holds no form value.
APIhpDateNav.mount(el, { onChange(iso, reason) }) → { set({ unit, value }), get() }; reason is prev · next · today · pick. Needs CALENDAR_JS before it.
